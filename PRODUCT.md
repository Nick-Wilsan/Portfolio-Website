# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Nick for Product Manager roles (internship / entry-level). They skim fast, compare candidates, and look for evidence of structured thinking, not just polished visuals.

Secondary: founders or small clients evaluating Nick for freelance product research, prioritization, or requirements-writing work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that proves Nick's product thinking through real artifacts (case studies, prioritization calls, written requirements) instead of claims. Success looks like a recruiter reading a case study and reaching out for an interview.

## Brand Personality

Evidence-based, defensible, calm. Confident without performing confidence: the writing and design should read like someone who already did the rigorous work and is now calmly presenting the conclusion, not someone trying to impress.

## Anti-references

The generic SaaS/startup template look: gradient hero text, uniform icon-grid feature cards, bento-box layouts, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section, reflexive 01/02/03 numbered scaffolding. This is an editorial, analytical portfolio, not a marketing site selling a product.

## Design Principles

- Show, don't tell. Every claim about process (research, prioritization, requirements) is backed by a visible artifact or a specific number, not an adjective.
- Calm confidence. Restrained color and motion; nothing loud enough to compete with the reasoning it's presenting.
- Person-first. The subject is Nick's thinking and voice, not a generic "product" being marketed. No face photos.
- Defend, don't decorate. Cut anything that doesn't help a recruiter evaluate the work faster.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum. All scroll/reveal motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (already implemented via `useReducedMotion` in several components; keep this standard for any new motion).
