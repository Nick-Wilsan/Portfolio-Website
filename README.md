## 📦 Products

### Hongfu System: production & warehouse system for a liquid manufacturer
Every drum gets a unique QR, every move is a scan, and accounting stays in Accurate Online.
- Discovery across 6 roles on the factory floor turned a brief of "labels, scanning, export" into one production flow replacing hand-built Excel plans and 4-part paper forms
- All 15 processes that write to Accurate sit behind a Super Admin approval queue, so finance can trust the integration
- Freelance, solo · Next.js · PostgreSQL · Drizzle · Accurate API · in daily use

[![Read the story](https://img.shields.io/badge/Read_the_story-1D6FA3?style=for-the-badge)](https://nickwilsan-portfolio.vercel.app/projects/hongfu-system)

### AkaMobilindo: used-car showroom app, live on Google Play
Fixed prices, standard specs, an installment estimate on every car, and chat with the showroom about that exact car.
- Browsing needs no login, because most buyers arrive from WhatsApp links
- Every car view is tagged by source channel for my thesis research
- Product owner & solo developer · React Native · Firebase · TypeScript

[![Read the story](https://img.shields.io/badge/Read_the_story-C8161D?style=for-the-badge)](https://nickwilsan-portfolio.vercel.app/projects/akamobilindo)
[![Google Play](https://img.shields.io/badge/Google_Play-414141?style=for-the-badge&logo=googleplay&logoColor=white)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.akamobilindo)
